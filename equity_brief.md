# Mental Health Outcomes | Illustrative Equity Brief

> **Portfolio simulation.** These are generated respondent records, not a Statistics Canada CCHS PUMF analysis. No reported percentages or demographic differences in this brief are official population estimates.

## Decision question
How might analysts examine mental health indicators across demographic groups without overstating what the data can support?

## Work demonstrated
- Constructed respondent-style sample data and prepared categorical indicators.
- Compared simulated reporting patterns by geography and sociodemographic categories.
- Designed a Power BI reporting workflow and summarized interpretation challenges.

## Important limitations
Generated respondent records are not a representative survey sample. They do not reproduce Statistics Canada's sampling design, survey weights or uncertainty measures. Results by Indigenous identity require special care about definitions, data governance and community interpretation. Model-based gaps cannot establish structural causes or justify population-specific policy conclusions.

## Next steps using real data
1. Obtain approved CCHS PUMF data and accompanying codebook.
2. Reconcile question wording and variable coding.
3. Apply relevant survey weights and variance methodology.
